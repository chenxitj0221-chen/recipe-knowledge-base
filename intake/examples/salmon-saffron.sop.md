## 二、制作步骤

### Saffron Sauce
1. Put the finely chopped onion with the wine in a small saucepan, heat until the wine is completely reduced.
   - 用料：onion 50 g；white wine 100 g
2. Add the butter and when it melts add the saffron, cook for a few seconds over high heat.
   - 用料：butter 75 g；saffron 0.5 g
3. Add the bomba rice. Continue cooking for a few more minutes and add the stock.
   - 用料：bomba paella rice 75 g；stock 350 g
4. Cook for 18 minutes, or until it starts to cook through.
5. Season with salt and pepper. Blend. Leave to cool and reserve.
   - 用料：salt（原文未给定量）；white pepper（原文未给定量）

### Sauteed Vegetables
6. Slice the vegetables very thinly. Add the grated ginger, salt, pepper and oil.
   - 用料：courgette 50 g；carrot 50 g；white cabbage 50 g；celery 50 g；spring onion 50 g；ginger 10 g；salt（原文未给定量）；pepper（原文未给定量）；olive oil（原文未给定量）
7. Leave the mixture to marinate for a minimum of 2 hours.
8. Saute in a very hot pan with oil for 2 minutes.
   - 用料：olive oil（原文未给定量）
9. Reserve. Serve al dente.

### 合成与装盘
10. Season the salmon and brush with oil.
   - 用料：salmon（原文未给定量）；oil（原文未给定量）
11. Seal the top part of the fish over a high heat, then cook the skin side.
   - 用料：salmon（原文未给定量）
12. Plate the vegetables, followed by the salmon, cover with sauce and finish with sprouts.
